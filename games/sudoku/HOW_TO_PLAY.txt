Fill the grid so every row, every column and every box holds each number exactly once.

## How to play
- The bold numbers are given and cannot change.
- Tap an empty cell to open the number picker, then tap a number. Clear empties the cell.
- Switch the picker to Notes to pencil in the numbers a cell might be.
- Tap any number, given or yours, to light up its row, column and box and every matching number.
- Undo takes back one step.
- Your puzzle is saved if you leave, so you can carry on later.

## Difficulty
Easy puzzles need only singles: a cell with one possible number, or a number with one possible cell. Medium and Hard need more advanced techniques, such as pairs.

## Settings
- Board size: 4 × 4, 6 × 6 or 9 × 9.
- Show mistakes: numbers that clash turn red.
- Remove notes when a number is placed.
- Highlight row, column, box and matching numbers.
