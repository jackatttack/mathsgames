Two players, one device. Pick tiles, then mix them to get closest to the target.

## Picking
- The target sits between the two scores.
- Take turns tapping a tile from the shared pool into your own zone, four each.
- Picks are final.

## Mixing
- At any time, merge tiles in your own zone: tap a tile, tap an operation corner, then tap another of your tiles.
- Your zone's Undo takes back merges. Double-tapping empty space in your zone does too.
- When you are happy, tap Lock in.

## Scoring
- Your tile closest to the target counts.
- The winner scores the winning margin, up to 10, plus 3 more for hitting the target exactly. A tie scores nothing.
- The results show the best answer each player's tiles could have made.
- The loser picks first next round. First to the match score wins.

## Settings
Choose the pool of numbers (Standard, Primes, Cubes, Factor-rich, All large or Small) and the score that wins the match.
