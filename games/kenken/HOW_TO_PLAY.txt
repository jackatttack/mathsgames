Fill the grid so every row and column holds each number once, and every cage makes its target.

## The rules
- On a 4 × 4 board use 1 to 4, on a 6 × 6 board use 1 to 6, and so on.
- Each cage is outlined in bold. Its label gives the target and the operation: 12× means the cage's numbers multiply to make 12.
- − and ÷ cages always have two cells: take the smaller from the larger, or divide the larger by the smaller.
- A cage with no operation is one cell. Its number is the answer.
- Numbers may repeat inside a cage, as long as they are not in the same row or column.

## How to play
- Tap a cell to open the number picker, then tap a number. Clear empties the cell.
- Switch the picker to Notes to pencil in the numbers a cell might be.
- Undo takes back one step.
- Your puzzle is saved if you leave, so you can carry on later.

## Settings
- Board size and which operations to use.
- Show mistakes: repeated numbers and wrong cages turn red.
