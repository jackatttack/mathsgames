Use the six numbers to make the target, like the TV numbers round.

## How to play
- Tap a number. Its four corners become + − × and ÷.
- Tap a corner, then tap a number that lights up to combine them.
- Every answer must be a positive whole number.
- You do not have to use every number.
- Reset puts the board back to the start.

## A run
A run is a set of boards against the clock. The banner shows the board number and your time. Skip moves on, but adds a time penalty. Your best time for each set of rules is kept.

## Settings
- How many big numbers to deal, or a mix.
- The skip penalty.
- Hints: tap the target to see the next step, then tap again to play it.
