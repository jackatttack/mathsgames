Four numbers are hidden. Ask questions to work out what they are.

## Asking
- Tap one of the two question cards to choose it.
- Tap the tiles it asks about, then tap Ask.
- The answer joins your clue log, and two new cards are dealt.

## Naming
- With no card chosen, tap a tile to open the number picker and name it.
- A wrong name costs a point.
- Switch the picker to Notes to pencil in the numbers a tile might be.

## Cracking the case
The case is cracked when every tile is named. Your score is compared with par for the case.

## Settings
Show what's still possible: shows how many answers remain and fades numbers already ruled out. Turn it off to do all the detective work yourself.
